Shelf & Tote Cut List

Four shelf units and two soldering totes, cut from the delivered Lowe's order. Sizes come from the 3D models, which follow the plans plus your changes. All dimensions are in inches. Length is along the grain.

1. Measure the delivered stock

Every size below recalculates from these numbers. The defaults are the values the model uses. Measure the actual sheets and milled stock with calipers before cutting, and type in what you find.

Fit rails, nosings and decks to the assembled frame. The plan says to trust the actual span over decimal precision. Fractions are rounded to the nearest 1/32.

Changes from the PDF plans

  • No caster pads (16) and no nosing glue tabs (24). The second 2×4 is now spare.
  • Casters screw up into the base from below. No through-bolts, nothing showing on the shelf.
  • All three rear rails stay: bottom rail, upper ledger, 1×2 top rail.
  • Tote handle is a 1 in round dowel in blind Forstner holes, replacing the ¾ × 1 rectangular handle. Corner cleats stay.
